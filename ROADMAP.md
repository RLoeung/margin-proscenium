# Current direction

- v0.15 is the deterministic multi-voice baseline.
- Next epoch: persistent literary state owned by Proscenium.
- Use decision-model/probabilistic inference for genuinely ambiguous literary decisions.
- Prefer deterministic processing where evidence is definitive.
- Persistence belongs to Proscenium, not to the decision model.
- Eventually separate narrator performance identity from first-person character dialogue identity.
- Evolve action cues into weighted evidence rather than automatically authoritative speaker assignments.
